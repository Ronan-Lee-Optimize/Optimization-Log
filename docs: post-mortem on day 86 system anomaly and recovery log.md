##  System Recovery Log: Managing Process Bottlenecks (June 28, 2026)

On day 86 of my daily optimization, I encountered my first system glitch in my Python learning journey. Due to extreme fatigue from my rigorous CSAT preparation routine, I missed the daily GitHub contribution deadline by 16 minutes, resulting in a single blank spot on my commit calendar.

As an aspiring Industrial Engineer, I chose to view this anomaly not as a failure, but as a critical data point for process optimization.

1. **The Bottleneck Identified:** Relying on late-night commits created a high-risk dependency on my remaining daily energy levels.
2. **The System Optimization:** On weekdays, my workflow for making code commits is highly reliable. Because I review and commit either before leaving the academy or immediately after arriving home, the defect rate—forgetting or failing due to fatigue—is practically zero. The intense environment of the academy naturally induces a high-focus state, and the physical activity of walking home maintains this optimal arousal level for at least 30 to 60 minutes post-arrival. Consequently, omitting a commit is highly improbable.

   However, Sundays, like yesterday, introduce a variance into this routine. I typically return home earlier, between 4:30 PM and 6:00 PM, for scheduled rest. This shift in the environment triggers procrastination driven by the desire to relax, significantly increasing the probability of a "human error" (such as forgetting to commit, as I did on Sunday). 
   
   To optimize this process and prevent future errors, I plan to leverage the forced focus state of the academy by mandating my Python study sessions either right before or immediately after heading home. Furthermore, recognizing that relying solely on human willpower is an inherently flawed system design, I have implemented a fail-safe mechanism: an automated alarm set for 6:15 PM to serve as an external trigger upon my return.

> **This single blank gap serves as a permanent architectural reminder:** A robust system does not just rely on willpower; it relies on proactive risk management. The streak resets, but the logic remains unbreakable.
